AE4cast Model System — AI Integrated at Every Step

Yield forecasting requires more than weather. AE4cast integrates AI across the full pipeline: weather trajectories, crop physiology, land classification, and econometric trend modeling — each step feeding into the next.

🌦
AI Weather Engine
Upgraded to a state-of-the-art AI foundation weather model generating 10-day ensemble trajectories at high resolution. Replaces single-trajectory autoregressive forecasts — more probabilistic information per day, calibrated against ERA5 reanalysis.
🌱
Crop Agronomy & Stress Model
Physics-informed yield response functions translate weather into crop impact: growing degree day accumulation, heat unit thresholds, and water deficit. Updated parameter estimates for 2026 using 40+ years of county-level data.
🛰
AI Crop Land Classification
Satellite imagery processed through AI classification models to identify crop type and active planting boundaries at county resolution. More accurate spatial area weights improve county-to-national aggregation — a key source of competitor error.
📊
Econometric Trend Integration
Separates structural yield trends (technology, genetics, management) from weather-driven deviations. This econometric layer is the source of AE4cast's minimum-bias advantage — weather-only models systematically under- or over-shoot as yields trend upward.
