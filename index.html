How It Works

AI Integrated at Every Step

Yield forecasting requires more than weather. AE4cast runs AI across the full pipeline — from raw weather to final yield signal.

Step 01
🌦
AI Weather Engine

10-day ensemble trajectories from a state-of-the-art AI foundation model at 0.1° resolution. Calibrated against ERA5 global reanalysis — the gold standard for historical weather.

▶
Step 02
🛰
AI Crop Land Classification

Satellite imagery processed through AI models to map crop type and planting boundaries at county resolution — more accurate spatial weights than any public dataset.

▶
Step 03
🌱
Crop Agronomy Model

Physics-informed functions translate weather into yield impact: growing degree days, heat stress thresholds, water deficit. Trained on 40+ years of county-level data.

▶
Step 04
📊
Econometric Integration

Separates structural yield trends from weather-driven deviations. This layer is the source of AE4cast's minimum-bias advantage over weather-only competitors.
