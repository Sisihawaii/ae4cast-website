Crop Intelligence Dashboard

Daily AE4cast yield forecasts for US corn and soybeans, side by side with USDA reports, crop condition and export flows. Preview release covering the 2024–2026 seasons. Request access below and we will e-mail your login.

What's inside

Forecast Daily state-level yield forecasts for corn and soybeans through the season, compared with USDA's monthly reports, plus extreme-heat and precipitation indices.
USDA Weekly planting progress and crop condition, planted-area changes, yield history versus USDA forecasts, and stocks-to-use ratios.
Exports GATS export volumes by destination and month, with weekly FAS export-sales commitments extending the latest months.
Download CSV downloads of forecasts, weather indices and USDA data for the seasons shown.

Preview release: the 2026 season forecasts run through September 3, 2026; a model update is in progress and the data will be refreshed. USDA and export data are updated through October 2026.
